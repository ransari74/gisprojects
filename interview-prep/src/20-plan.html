The rule that makes this work

Reading a design is worth about a fifth of drawing one. Every topic day ends with fifteen minutes at a blank page: redraw the day's architecture from memory, then diff it against the section. The diff is your gap list, and the gap list is what week 4 is for.

Daily template

BlockTimeWhat you actually do
Warm-up10 minRe-derive yesterday's back-of-envelope numbers from memory. No notes.
Algorithms60–75 minOne pattern. Read the template once, then solve 4–6 problems on a timer — 25 min hard cap, then read the solution and re-implement it from scratch the next morning.
Design read45 minOne section here, plus one real engineering blog post on the same topic.
Blank page15 minRedraw it from memory. Speak the design out loud as you draw — the mouth is the muscle being trained, not the hand.
Log5 minThree lines: what you got wrong, what you'd say differently, one number you forgot.
